01

THE IDEA

Crypto has an identity problem.

Crypto projects spend years building names, logos, websites, and social accounts. But the most permanent part of a project is often ignored: the contract address.

A contract address is more than a string. It contains origin, transaction history, ownership history, and liquidity history. It is the permanent fingerprint of a project.

Yet the most important identifier in crypto is still treated as nothing more than random characters.

We believe this can change.
02
